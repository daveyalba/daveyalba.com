I’m a technology reporter for Bloomberg in New York, covering Big Tech and its power. Before joining Bloomberg in 2022, I covered online disinformation for The New York Times, and before that I was a senior technology reporter for BuzzFeed News. I’ve been a reporter for Wired in Silicon Valley and an editor and writer at Popular Mechanics, and I’ve written for The Wirecutter, IEEE Spectrum and Gizmodo. I studied science writing at Columbia University.

At Bloomberg, my colleagues and I have won two Online Journalism Awards for excellence in technology reporting, in 2023 and 2024, for investigations into the post-Roe information crisis and bias in generative AI. The post-Roe work also won a 2023 Deadline Club Award, and our reporting on how social media set the stage for right-wing political victories was a 2025 Online Journalism Awards finalist. At the Times, our reporting on pay-for-play local news networks received an honorable mention at the 2020 SABEW Awards.

In 2019, I won the Livingston Award for international reporting and a Mirror Award in a special category, Journalism in Peril, for my reported feature, “How Duterte Used Facebook To Fuel the Philippine Drug War.”
